My name is Meredith Onions, and I am master's student in informatics at the University of Iowa. In May 2026, I completed my Bachelor of Arts in Informatics with a graphic design cognate alongside a double minor in computer science and sociology.


I worked as an undergraduate Academic Research Assistant at an Iowa human-computer interaction lab before becoming a Graduate Research Assistant this academic year. In the summer of 2025, I worked at Leepfrog Technologies as a Data Visualization Development Intern, where I utilized D3.js to make complex visualizations and animations. This past summer, I interned at Exact Sciences as a Web Product Manager on the Customer Experience team.


I am very interested in developing usable technology; I particularly love conducting research and turning complex information into a digestible product. I developed this website to present some more about me and my work.
