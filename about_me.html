My name is Meredith Onions, and I am a senior at the University of Iowa studying informatics with an art cognate alongside a double minor in computer science and sociology. This means that I am obtaining relevant skills in data analysis and computing as well as usability principles and graphic design.


For 1.5 years now, I have worked as an academic research assistant within a university lab that is primarily focused on human-computer interaction. This has allowed me to gain valuable analytical skills when it comes to UX.


Also, this past summer, I worked as a Data Visualization Development Intern at Leepfrog Technologies in Coralville, IA. I learned and utilized D3.JS to implement new features and improve previous ones within CourseLeaf, Leepfrog's primary software product. This experience taught me a lot about creating interfaces that are both complex and user-friendly.


I am very interested in the field of web development. I want to innovate and solve problems in the world of technology, and I want to make the world a better place in doing so. I developed this website to present some more about me and my work.
